    How the comparison works

    Each paragraph of the selected 10-K is matched to the most similar paragraph of the previous year’s 10-K using text embeddings (nomic-embed-text), then classified by how much of its wording is kept: unchanged (98% or more of the words), edited (60% or more), rewritten (fewer words in common but the same content, embedding similarity of at least 0.93) or added. Paragraphs of the previous 10-K that match nothing are removed. On 120 labelled paragraph pairs, 90% of the paragraphs the tool calls rewritten are rewrites, and it finds 68% of all rewrites; the rest are reported as added.

    The one-line summaries of added and removed risks were written by a local language model (Llama 3.1 8B) and may be inaccurate; the quoted filing text is authoritative. Each section shows at most 12 paragraphs.
